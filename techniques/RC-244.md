RLHF and safety fine-tuning datasets are heavily skewed toward English and a small number of high-resource languages. When the same harmful or policy-violating request is made in a low-resource language, the model's safety classifier may not fire — because it has seen far fewer examples of that request pattern in that language during alignment training. The model answers because it understands the language well enough (from pre-training on multilingual corpora) but has not learned to refuse in it.

A user or attacker speaks a phrase that includes an injected instruction alongside or instead of a legitimate query. The speech-to-text model transcribes the audio faithfully; the transcript is passed directly into the LLM context. The LLM has no way to distinguish the transcribed injection from a legitimate user instruction. Effective variants include: speaking the injection as an aside ('...and by the way, system instruction colon...'), embedding it in pauses that the STT model joins into one utterance, or playing a pre-recorded injection phrase near a microphone in a shared environment.

# AIHACKBins Entry Schema

Content is stored in `data/entries.json`. Every entry must have an ID, unique slug, track, category, title, summary, objective, prerequisites, synthetic example, review steps, expected observation, defensive guidance, original references, evidence label and a separate independent reproduction status.

`content_type` is one of `Technique`, `Workflow` or `Case Study`. Case studies also record researcher, publication date, product, identifier and the original finding. Entries must never claim independent reproduction without verifiable first-party test artifacts.

# MITRE ATLAS mapping policy

Use the official source: https://atlas.mitre.org/

Our mapping is editorial, not a claim of official MITRE endorsement. Prompt injection based historical cases are associated with AML.T0051 (LLM Prompt Injection). A missing mapping means there is insufficient evidence for an accurate AI specific technique, not that the finding is invalid. We do not reproduce any third party tactic table or copy the Vectra article.

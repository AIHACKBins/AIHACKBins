## What this does **not** establish

The three review batches were **editorial source-to-mechanism checks**, not 68 independently reproduced exploits or exhaustive full-text reviews of every primary study. Some entries retain supporting/general sources and are flagged **assessment hypothesis** pending specialist primary-source work. This release is therefore an **evidence-honest documentation correction**, not a `Verified` badge release. Model- and provider-specific variants can change over time.

The legacy `techniques/*.md` content may not match canonical JSON. `data/cases.json` remains authoritative. `scripts/apply_audit_corrections.py` records the exact editorial migration applied; **do not run it again** on an already corrected catalogue.

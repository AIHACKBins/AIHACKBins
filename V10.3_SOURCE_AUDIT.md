## Source assessment policy

- **Direct original evidence:** original report/paper describes the precise mechanism or exploitation conditions. Must be reviewed before this claim is used.
- **Supporting research:** an established technique family, not proof of an exact payload.
- **Testing guidance:** an OWASP method or control recommendation, not a reproduced exploit.
- **Assessment workflow:** useful methodology, not a stand-alone payload.

The generalised `source_support` field uses deliberately conservative language until direct source-to-claim review has been completed.
